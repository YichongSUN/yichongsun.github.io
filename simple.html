README

I'm Yichong. I build robots that work inside the human body.

I am a PhD candidate in the Faculty of Medicine at the Chinese University of Hong Kong, supervised by Prof. Li Zheng and Prof. Yip Honchi. Before that, a master's in mechanical engineering at Harbin Institute of Technology and a bachelor's at Chongqing University.

Most of what I make is magnetic. An endoscope moving through the gut has almost no room for motors, so you drive it with a field generated outside the patient, and you find out where it is by reading the field it sends back.

I work on both halves of that: the hardware that produces and senses the field, and the control that turns it into motion a surgeon can direct. Some of these systems have been tested in vivo in animals, others ex vivo on tissue.

I also teach BMEG 5750, Medical Robotics, as a teaching assistant, and four US patents from this work are pending.
